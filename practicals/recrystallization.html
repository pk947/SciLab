Recrystallization

ප්‍රතිස්ඵටිකීකරණය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

A solid is purified using its higher solubility in a hot solvent than in a cold one: dissolve it in the minimum hot solvent, filter hot, cool slowly so a pure crystal lattice forms and soluble impurities stay in solution, then filter and dry.

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: beaker, hot plate, funnel & filter paper, Buchner funnel, ice bath.

Step-by-step procedure (5 steps)

  1. Choose a solvent: solid insoluble cold, soluble hot.
  2. Dissolve the sample in a minimum of hot solvent.
  3. Hot-filter out insoluble particles.
  4. Cool slowly so pure crystals grow.
  5. Vacuum-filter, wash with cold solvent and dry.

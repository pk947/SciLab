Principle of Moments

ඝූර්ණ මූලධර්මය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

For a body in equilibrium, the sum of clockwise moments about any point equals the sum of anticlockwise moments: W₁L₁ = W₂L₂, so W₂ = W₁L₁/L₂.

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: metre rule, knife-edge, known mass W₁, unknown mass W₂, light thread.

Step-by-step procedure (5 steps)

  1. Balance the bare rule on the knife-edge and mark the centre (50 cm).
  2. Hang the known weight W₁ on the left and unknown W₂ on the right.
  3. Vary L₁ until the rule is horizontal; read L₁ and L₂.
  4. Press “Record” — repeat for at least 5 different values of L₂.
  5. Calculate W₂ = W₁L₁/L₂ for each reading and take the mean.

The simulation guides you through each of these steps, lets you change the variables, and finishes with a short quiz.

Column Chromatography

තීරු වර්ණාවලේඛනය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

A mixture is loaded on top of a column of silica (stationary phase) and washed down with solvent (mobile phase). Compounds that stick less to the silica (less polar) travel faster and elute first; fractions are collected from the stopcock.

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: glass column, silica gel, stopcock, solvent, retort stand, collection flasks.

Step-by-step procedure (4 steps)

  1. Pack the column with silica and load the mixture on top.
  2. Add solvent and open the stopcock.
  3. Watch the bands separate and move down at different speeds.
  4. Collect each band as it elutes; never let the column run dry.

Law of Parallelogram of Forces

බල සමාන්තරාස්‍ර නියමය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

If two forces acting at a point are represented in magnitude and direction by two adjacent sides of a parallelogram drawn from that point, their resultant is represented by the diagonal through the same point.

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: parallelogram board, pair of pulleys, known loads & pans, light inextensible threads, white paper & pins, metre rule, set square, pencil.

Step-by-step procedure (7 steps)

  1. Set the board vertical; pulleys with minimal friction.
  2. Pin white paper on the board.
  3. Join three threads at a knot, pass two over the pulleys and hang loads P, Q and S.
  4. Adjust the loads (sliders) until the knot is at rest.
  5. Mark the thread directions on the paper (pins + pencil).
  6. Complete the parallelogram to a scale — press the button.
  7. Check the diagonal equals S in size and is opposite in direction.

The simulation guides you through each of these steps, lets you change the variables, and finishes with a short quiz.

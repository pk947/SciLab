Low-pressure (Vacuum) Distillation

අඩු පීඩන ආසවනය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

A liquid boils when its vapour pressure equals the external pressure, so lowering the pressure lowers the boiling point. Heat-sensitive or high-boiling liquids can be distilled without decomposing.

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: flask, capillary bleed, vacuum pump, manometer, condenser, receiver.

Step-by-step procedure (4 steps)

  1. Seal the apparatus; the capillary bleed prevents bumping.
  2. Start the pump and read the pressure on the manometer.
  3. Heat: the liquid boils far below its normal boiling point.
  4. Release the vacuum slowly before stopping the pump.

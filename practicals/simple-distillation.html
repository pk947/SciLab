Simple Distillation

සරල ආසවනය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

Heating vaporises the lower-boiling component; the vapour is cooled in a water-cooled condenser and collected as distillate. Use it to separate a liquid from a dissolved solid (water from salt water) or liquids whose boiling points differ by more than ~50 °C.

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: round-bottom flask, burner, boiling chips, thermometer, condenser, receiver.

Step-by-step procedure (4 steps)

  1. Put salt water and boiling chips in the flask; fit the thermometer at the side arm.
  2. Turn the condenser water ON (in at the bottom, out at the top).
  3. Heat gently; note the steady vapour temperature (~100 °C).
  4. Collect the distillate; stop before the flask runs dry.

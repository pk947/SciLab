Soxhlet Extraction – Adathodai Alkaloids

සොක්ස්ලට් නිස්සාරණය – ආඩතෝඩා ඇල්කලොයිඩ

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

Fresh solvent condenses into the thimble holding Adathodai leaf powder; when the chamber fills, it siphons back to the flask carrying dissolved vasicine. Each cycle starts with pure solvent, keeping the concentration gradient (Fick’s law) high. Alkaloids stay in the flask; solvent re-evaporates.

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: heating mantle, flask with methanol/ethanol, Soxhlet extractor, thimble, condenser.

Step-by-step procedure (4 steps)

  1. Pack dried, crushed Adathodai leaves in the thimble.
  2. Heat the solvent; vapour rises and condenses onto the powder.
  3. The chamber fills, then siphons back to the flask (one cycle).
  4. Repeat cycles; the flask solution darkens as alkaloids accumulate.

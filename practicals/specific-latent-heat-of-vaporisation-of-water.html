Specific Latent Heat of Vaporisation of Water

ජලයේ වාෂ්පීකරණයේ විශිෂ්ට ගුප්ත තාපය

Overview and theory

Specific latent heat of vaporisation L_v is the heat needed to change 1 kg of liquid to vapour at its boiling point. Dry steam at 100 °C is condensed in cool water: heat gained by water + calorimeter = heat lost by steam.

Apparatus and key points

(m_w c_w + m₁c_c)(θ₂ − θ₁) = m_s L_v + m_s c_w (100 − θ₂)Apparatus: boiler, burner, steam trap, calorimeter with lagging, lid, thermometer, stirrer, balance.

Step-by-step procedure (6 steps)

  1. Weigh the calorimeter (m₁) and with water (m₂); read θ₁.
  2. Light the burner and boil the water in the boiler.
  3. When steam is steady, pass dry steam (via the steam trap) into the water; stir.
  4. Stop when the temperature has risen by about 15 °C; read θ₂.
  5. Weigh again (m₃); steam mass m_s = m₃ − m₂.
  6. Calculate L_v.

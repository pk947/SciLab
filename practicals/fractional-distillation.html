Fractional Distillation

භාගික ආසවනය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

Vapour rises through a fractionating column where it repeatedly condenses and re-evaporates, enriching the more volatile component at the top. It is used when boiling points differ by less than ~25–70 °C.

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: round-bottom flask, fractionating column, thermometer, condenser, receivers, burner.

Step-by-step procedure (4 steps)

  1. Fill the flask with an ethanol–water mixture; fit the column.
  2. Turn the condenser water ON and heat gently.
  3. Thermometer holds ~78 °C: collect fraction 1 (ethanol).
  4. Temperature rises to 100 °C: change receiver for fraction 2 (water).

Caffeine Extraction from Waste Tea

අපද්‍රව්‍ය තේවලින් කැෆේන් නිස්සාරණය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

Caffeine is separated from waste tea in stages. Hot water dissolves caffeine and tannins; basic lead acetate precipitates the tannins and colouring matter; dilute H₂SO₄ removes the excess lead as PbSO₄; the clear solution is shaken with CHCl₃, in which caffeine is much more soluble than in water; evaporating the CHCl₃ leaves crystalline caffeine.

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: beakers, hot plate, conical flask, funnel & filter paper, separating funnel with stand, basic lead acetate, dil. H₂SO₄, chloroform. Chloroform is toxic: work in a fume cupboard.

Step-by-step procedure (8 steps)

  1. Boil waste tea with water
  2. Filter the hot extract
  3. Add basic lead acetate
  4. Filter off the precipitate
  5. Add dil. H₂SO₄
  6. Filter again
  7. Extract with CHCl₃
  8. Evaporate the CHCl₃

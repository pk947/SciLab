Specific Latent Heat of Fusion of Ice

අයිස් ද්‍රවීකරණයේ විශිෂ්ට ගුප්ත තාපය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

Specific latent heat of fusion L_f is the heat needed to change 1 kg of ice at 0 °C into water at 0 °C. Mixing method: heat lost by warm water + calorimeter = heat to melt ice + heat to warm melted ice to θ₂.

Apparatus and key points

(m_w c_w + m₁c_c)(θ₁ − θ₂) = m_i L_f + m_i c_w θ₂Apparatus: copper calorimeter & stirrer, electronic balance, thermometer, dry ice cubes, filter paper, warm water.

Step-by-step procedure (6 steps)

  1. Weigh the empty, dry calorimeter (m₁).
  2. Half-fill with warm water (5–10 °C above room) and weigh again (m₂); m_w = m₂ − m₁.
  3. Read the initial temperature θ₁.
  4. Add dried ice cubes and stir until all the ice melts.
  5. Read the lowest temperature θ₂ and weigh (m₃); m_i = m₃ − m₂.
  6. Calculate L_f.

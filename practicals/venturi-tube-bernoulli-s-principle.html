Venturi Tube – Bernoulli’s Principle

වෙන්චුරි නළය – බර්නූලි මූලධර්මය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

In steady horizontal flow, A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ (continuity) and p + ½ρv² = constant. Where the tube is narrow the speed is higher and pressure (piezometer water level) is lower.

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: Venturi tube, piezometer tubes, water supply, flow meter.

Step-by-step procedure (5 steps)

  1. Let water flow steadily through the horizontal tube.
  2. Read the water levels in piezometers A (wide), B (narrow), C (wide).
  3. Level in A is higher than in B: pressure is lower where speed is higher.
  4. Increase the flow rate and note how Δh grows.
  5. Compute v₁ from Δh and compare with Q/A₁.

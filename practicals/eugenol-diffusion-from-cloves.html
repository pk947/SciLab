Eugenol Diffusion from Cloves

කරාබුනැටිවලින් යූජිනෝල් විසරණය

▶ Open the interactive 3D simulation

Overview and theory

Eugenol (60–90 % of clove oil, b.p. ≈ 250 °C) would decompose if heated directly. Boiling cloves with water co-distils it with steam just below 100 °C; the cloudy distillate holds free eugenol droplets that are extracted and analysed (e.g. by IR).

Apparatus and key points

Apparatus: 100 mL flask, cloves (~3 g), water, glass bead, condenser, ice-bath receiver.

Step-by-step procedure (4 steps)

  1. Weigh ~3 g of cloves; add 35–40 mL water and soak for 15 min.
  2. Set up simple distillation; receiver in an ice bath.
  3. Start condenser water; heat to a steady rate (avoid bumping).
  4. Collect the cloudy distillate; extract and characterise eugenol.

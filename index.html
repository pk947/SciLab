    SciLab – Virtual A/L Physics and Chemistry Practicals in 3D

    SciLab is an online virtual science laboratory for Advanced Level (A/L) students. Every practical runs as an interactive 3D simulation in your browser, so you can practise without lab equipment, chemicals or any download. Follow the step-by-step procedure, change the controls, read the theory, and test yourself with a short quiz. All content is available in English and Sinhala.

    Physics practicals

    6 practicals covering mechanics, fluids and heat: verify the law of parallelogram of forces and the principle of moments, explore Bernoulli’s principle with a Venturi tube, test Archimedes’ principle, and measure the specific latent heats of fusion and vaporisation.

    • Law of Parallelogram of Forces – Balance three forces on a board and show that the diagonal of the force parallelogram equals the resultant.
    • Principle of Moments – Balance loads on a metre rule and verify that clockwise and anticlockwise moments are equal.
    • Venturi Tube – Bernoulli’s Principle – See how water speed and pressure change along a Venturi tube and verify Bernoulli’s principle.
    • Specific Latent Heat of Fusion of Ice – Measure the specific latent heat of fusion of ice using the method of mixtures.
    • Specific Latent Heat of Vaporisation of Water – Condense dry steam in cool water to measure the specific latent heat of vaporisation.
    • Archimedes’ Principle – Compare weights in air and in water to verify that buoyant force equals the weight of fluid displaced.

    Chemistry practicals

    13 practicals on separation, purification and extraction: simple, steam, fractional and low-pressure distillation, recrystallization, thin-layer and column chromatography, sublimation, extraction of caffeine, eugenol and alkaloids, plus soap making and biodiesel preparation.

    • Simple Distillation – Separate a liquid from a mixture using heating, a water-cooled condenser and a collecting flask.
    • Steam Distillation – Extract heat-sensitive, water-immiscible oils by distilling them with steam below 100 °C.
    • Fractional Distillation – Separate liquids with close boiling points using a fractionating column.
    • Low-pressure (Vacuum) Distillation – Lower the pressure to distil heat-sensitive or high-boiling liquids at a lower temperature.
    • Recrystallization – Purify a solid by dissolving it in hot solvent, filtering hot and cooling slowly to form pure crystals.
    • Thin-Layer Chromatography (TLC) – Separate the components of a mixture on a silica plate and calculate their Rf values.
    • Column Chromatography – Separate compounds by washing a mixture down a silica column and collecting the fractions.
    • Sublimation Purification – Purify a solid that turns directly into vapour, such as ammonium chloride or camphor.
    • Caffeine Extraction from Waste Tea – Extract caffeine from waste tea in stages: hot water, tannin removal, then solvent extraction.
    • Eugenol Diffusion from Cloves – Isolate eugenol from cloves by steam co-distillation and extract it from the distillate.
    • Soxhlet Extraction – Adathodai Alkaloids – Use a Soxhlet apparatus to extract vasicine from Adathodai leaf powder with fresh solvent each cycle.
    • Soap Making (Saponification) – Make soap by heating oil with ethanol and sodium hydroxide, and learn how saponification works.
    • Biodiesel from Vegetable Oil – Prepare biodiesel by transesterification of vegetable oil with methanol and a base catalyst.

    How SciLab helps you learn

    • Interactive 3D simulations – rotate, zoom and adjust each experiment as if you were at the bench.
    • Step-by-step procedures – every practical lists the exact steps and apparatus.
    • Theory notes – the key principles and formulas behind each experiment.
    • Quizzes – check your understanding after each practical.
    • English and Sinhala – switch language at any time.
    • Create your own lab – build and save a practical of your own.

    How to use a practical

    1. Choose a Physics or Chemistry practical from the list above.
    2. Read the theory and the list of steps.
    3. Use the controls to run the simulation and record your observations.
    4. Take the quiz to review what you learned.
